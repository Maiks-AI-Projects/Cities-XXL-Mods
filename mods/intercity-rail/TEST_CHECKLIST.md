# Deferred In-Game Test Checklist

Use this only after returning home and creating a disposable Cities XXL test world.

1. Back up existing Cities XXL saves.
2. Close the game and copy `dist/zzz_PersonalIntercityRail_0_1_0.patch` into the appropriate local `Paks` mod location.
3. Create a new world on a map with a visible rail city-link corridor.
4. Confirm that the railway and station appear in the construction menus.
5. Place a short isolated railway and watch for pedestrians or road vehicles.
6. Place one station and record passenger and freight capacity before and after placement.
7. Extend the railway to the valid map edge and confirm whether a connector appears.
8. Save, exit, reload, and check the station, railway, connector, and resource values.
9. Open or create a second city and attempt passenger and freight trades.
10. Demolish the edge connector, a middle rail segment, and the station separately; record when capacity disappears.
11. Place a second station and record whether the original capacity stacks.
12. Remove the patch only after leaving the disposable test world.

Please preserve screenshots and the latest game log after any crash or unexpected result.
