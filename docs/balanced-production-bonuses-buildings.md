# Balanced Production Bonuses: affected buildings

These values were extracted directly from the verified vanilla Cities XXL 1.5.0
`data.pak` and its English localization archive. The mod changes only
`MaxMonthlyCost` and `UpkeepCost`; all production bonuses remain unchanged.

- **Original maximum** is the vanilla `MaxMonthlyCost` value shown/used as the building's monthly budget ceiling.
- **Original upkeep** is the vanilla base `UpkeepCost` used by the simulation.
- **Mod maximum** and **mod upkeep** are both 50% of their original values.
